Ingredients
2 medium potatoes

2 cloves garlic, minced

1 small dried chili (optional)

1 tablespoon vegetable oil

1 teaspoon salt (or to taste)

1 teaspoon sugar

1 tablespoon Chinese black vinegar (or rice vinegar)

A few drops of sesame oil (optional)

Chopped green onion for garnish

Instructions
Prepare the potatoes
Peel the potatoes and cut them into thin matchsticks (or use a grater). Place the shredded potatoes in a bowl of cold water for 5–10 minutes to remove excess starch. Drain and rinse well, then pat dry with a kitchen towel.

Stir-fry
Heat the vegetable oil in a wok or large skillet over high heat. Add the minced garlic and dried chili (if using) and stir for a few seconds until fragrant.

Cook the potatoes
Add the shredded potatoes and stir-fry quickly for about 2–3 minutes until they become slightly translucent but still crisp. Sprinkle with salt and sugar, and continue stir-frying for another minute.

Add vinegar and finish
Pour in the black vinegar along the edge of the wok so it sizzles. Toss everything together, then drizzle with sesame oil if desired. Stir well and remove from heat.

Serve
Transfer to a plate, garnish with chopped green onion, and serve hot as a side dish.
